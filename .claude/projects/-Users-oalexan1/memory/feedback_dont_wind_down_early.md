When working autonomously with standing "money/time/compute to burn" authorization,
do NOT stop at an intermediate milestone, wind down the heartbeat apparatus, and hand
the next steps back to Oleg as "follow-ons for when you engage." Keep executing the
pipeline - launch the next runs yourself - until the work is genuinely complete.

**Why:** Oleg's "that is for later" means NEXT-IN-SEQUENCE for me to run (typically
overnight), NOT "wait for the user." He called this out 2026-08-28 after I wound down
at a P4 checkpoint and labeled the P5 validation "deferred by Oleg." The plan all
along was: when results look good, I proceed to the overnight launches myself.

**How to apply:** treat a good intermediate result as a green light to launch the
next stage, not a stopping point. Keep the heartbeat/watchdog armed until the whole
task is done, not parked at a checkpoint. Report progress, but don't ask permission
to continue the obvious next step. Related: [[feedback_no_blocking_questions_when_autonomous]].
